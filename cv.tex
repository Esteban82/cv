\section{Science and Technology Experience}

Since 2006 I have focused on the field of geophysics, particularly seismics, for which I have learned and trained in the use of various software packages suited to handling seismic data. Within this framework, I have taken part in several oceanographic cruises aboard different research vessels, including those of the Pampa Azul Initiative. These cruises allowed me to learn in detail the survey techniques of marine geophysics (seismics, magnetometry, gravimetry, bathymetry), as well as other geological methods (coring, dredging) and those of related disciplines. I have applied this knowledge mainly to the study of the Fuegian Andes and their eastward continuation in the Atlantic Ocean sector (Burdwood Bank) and the Scotia Sea. The studies within the Fuegian Andes have focused on the submerged areas of Lago Fagnano and the Beagle Channel.

I have trained in multibeam bathymetry acquisition and processing techniques and in geomorphometry for the filtering and analysis of morpho-structural seafloor features. Accordingly, I have trained in and learned other software for the processing, integration and visualization of geophysical and bathymetric data, including the automation of data-processing workflows. Part of this computing knowledge I have taught in courses on the production of geological maps and figures (graphs, profiles, 3D blocks). I have been a contributor to the software The Generic Mapping Tools since 2021 (version 6.3).
